# Drip

**Open-source email outreach CRM**

Drip helps small teams manage contacts and outreach campaigns in one place. Connect your own Gmail, Outlook, or SMTP mailbox; schedule follow-ups; rotate sending accounts; and track delivery, replies, bounces, and campaign activity.
